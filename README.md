## Plot Summary

*Stranger Things* is set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana. The story begins with the mysterious disappearance of a young boy named Will Byers. As his friends and family search for him, they uncover a secret government experiment, supernatural forces, and a strange girl with psychokinetic abilities known as Eleven.

### Why I Love It

- **Nostalgia:** The show perfectly captures 80s pop culture and aesthetics.  
- **Suspense:** Each episode keeps you on the edge of your seat.  
- **Characters:** Deep, relatable characters with strong friendships.  
- **Supernatural Elements:** The Upside Down is a fascinating and creepy alternate dimension.
